## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Work is a rite. Rest is earned.

Pomodoro of the Damned is a focus timer built for people who take their
productivity seriously — and their aesthetics darker. No ads, no accounts,
no tracking, no nonsense. Just a timer, done right.

FIVE RESEARCHED RITES
Choose from five of the most-cited Pomodoro interval ratios, each with its
source:
• The Classic Rite — 25 min work / 5 min break (Francesco Cirillo's
  original Pomodoro Technique)
• The Golden Ratio — 52 min work / 17 min break (DeskTime's study of its
  most productive users)
• The Scribe's Hour — 50 min work / 10 min break (a popular deep-work and
  long-form writing ratio)
• The Ultradian Vigil — 90 min work / 20 min break (built on ultradian
  rhythm research)
• The Novice's Trial — 15 min work / 5 min break (a low-friction ratio for
  building the habit)

Or forge your own: set custom work, short-break, and long-break minutes,
and how many trials until a long break.

BUILT FOR ACTUALLY WORKING
• Large, legible countdown with an ember progress ring you can read at a
  glance
• A tolling bell and a vibration when a phase ends — no need to keep
  checking the screen
• Keeps your screen awake while a trial is running
• Tracks how many trials you've endured

NO DATA LEAVES YOUR DEVICE
Pomodoro of the Damned collects nothing. No analytics, no ad SDKs, no
account, no server it talks to. Your settings and session tally live only
in your device's local storage. Full privacy policy:
https://grimdoro.com/privacy.html

Work is a rite. Rest is earned. Begin.
```

## Content rating questionnaire — expected answers

The app has zero objectionable content, no user-generated content, no
violence, no gambling-style mechanics, and the "grimdark" theming is purely
aesthetic flavor text (no gore/horror imagery). Answer "No" to essentially
every content question. Expect a rating equivalent to **Everyone / PEGI 3**.
The one thing worth a second look: the "Damned"/skull-adjacent flavor
language (e.g. phase names like "The Long Night") — this is mild gothic
styling, not violent or scary content, but mention it honestly if a
question asks about "fear themes" or similar.

## Data safety form — expected answers

No data is collected or shared, full stop. Every category (location,
personal info, financial, health, messages, photos, device IDs, usage
analytics, etc.) should be answered "No data collected." This matches
`privacy.html` exactly — keep them in sync if anything about the app's
behavior changes later.
