Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Pomodoro of the Damned does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, from you or anyone else. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server this app talks to at all.

What stays on your device

  • Your selected timer preset or custom minutes
  • Your sound and "keep screen awake" toggle choices
  • Your completed-session tally

All of the above is stored using your browser's local storage, on your device only. It is never sent anywhere — not to us, not to any third party. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data for grimdoro.com erases it completely.

Permissions this app may request

  • Screen Wake Lock — keeps your screen from sleeping while a timer is running. Can be turned off in-app at any time.
  • Vibration — a short vibration when a phase ends. Standard device feature, nothing is recorded.
  • Notifications (Android app only, where applicable) — used solely to alert you locally when a work or rest phase ends.

None of these permissions involve collecting or transmitting data of any kind.

Third parties

This app loads two web fonts from Google Fonts at runtime (for the typography). No other third-party service, SDK, or tracker is used.

Children's privacy

Since no data is collected from anyone, this includes children. The app is appropriate for general audiences.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: contact@grimdoro.com
